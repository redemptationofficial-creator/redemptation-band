About Us

We are a creative collective of like-minded individuals. We are united by style and love for music.

Each of our songs is a separate story. A story that happened to one of us. Or to someone else — but we heard it, passed it through ourselves, and told it in music.

We don't write custom songs. But we can create a unique musical dedication for you — if your story resonates with us as strongly as our own would.

Why we work remotely. Our team works remotely. Not because we are hiding something — but because our members live in different cities and even countries: St. Petersburg, Moscow, Novosibirsk, Kazan, Minsk, London. Thousands of kilometers separate us, but one thing unites us: music. That is why we don't give concerts yet. But this doesn't stop us from creating what touches hearts.

About personal things. We have provided all the necessary information about us. Everything else is our private life, which is not subject to comment.

Why REDEMPTATION

Initially, the project was supposed to be called simply Redemption — "Atonement". This word accurately reflected the essence: the path from falling to rebirth, from pain to light.

But when Michael Matthew — our vocalist from London — joined the project, he suggested another name. He combined two words: Redemption and Temptation.

Thus REDEMPTATION was born — a neologism in which both forces live: light and darkness, choice and temptation, fall and rebellion. This name more accurately reflects what we sing about.

The name was suggested by Michael. We accepted it unanimously.

Tell Us Your Story

We create musical dedications. Not "custom songs" — but music in which someone's destiny lives.

To write such a thing, we must live your story. Pass it through ourselves. Only then is born what touches the soul.

We don't take on every story. Not because we choose the "worthy" — but because we cannot work without a response. If the story doesn't resonate with us, we won't be able to create what resonates with you.

If you want a dedication — write to us. Tell us your story.

We will read it. We will reply to everyone. If we feel it's ours — we'll say "yes". If not — we'll honestly say so. No offense. No omissions.

We work at our own pace. We don't chase deadlines to the detriment of quality. If you are ready to wait — we will be glad. If you need it "tomorrow" — we most likely won't be able to help. But we will honestly say so right away.
